A few things we ask

Keep the world worth sharing.

These are simple guidelines, not a wall of fine print. Use good judgment, respect other players, and help keep NexonMC a place people want to return to.

  1. Be respectful. Treat players and staff like people. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not welcome.
  2. Protect other players' work. Do not grief, steal, or use someone else's builds without permission.
  3. Play fair. No cheating, exploits, hacked clients, or anything that gives an unfair advantage.
  4. Keep chat comfortable. Avoid spam, excessive advertising, and content that makes the community difficult to enjoy.
  5. Ask when unsure. Reach out in Discord before acting if a situation or server rule is unclear.
